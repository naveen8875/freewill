# Community conduct

Freewill should be an enjoyable place to learn, experiment, and compare results.

Be curious, specific, and respectful. Critique code, methods, and evidence rather than people. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or expose private information about another contributor. Do not present generated output as proof of consciousness or use the project to make unsupported claims about people or groups.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and community participation that undermine a safe, constructive project. Serious concerns should be reported privately through the repository's security or maintainer contact channel.
