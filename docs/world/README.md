# World systems

Freewill's world systems share one rule: outcomes must come from reachable people, conserved resources, elapsed time, and recorded consequences.

- [Wilderness slice](wilderness-slice.md) — complete empty-hand survival loop.
- [Physical world](physical-world.md) — resources, crops, fire, water, health, roads, repair, and demolition.
- [Settlement systems](settlement-systems.md) — skills, production chains, buildings, projects, escrow, and votes.

## Social and institutional systems

Citizens can request, share, steal, barter, rescue, teach, warn, conceal, claim, challenge, promise, repay, forgive, retaliate, form households, and create or leave voluntary groups.

Groups begin informal. Consensus, majority, leader, or council systems can be adopted later. Public projects, laws, currency, businesses, factions, and rebellion require their own prerequisites and choices; they are not universal spawn rules.

## Causality

A world event should answer:

- Who acted?
- What did they know?
- Which legal options existed?
- What did they choose?
- Where did it occur?
- Which resources and time did it consume?
- Who experienced the consequence?
- Which later memory or relationship change resulted?

The in-game decision map and chronicle expose this chain.
